Every cat in this colony has received veterinary care thanks to the generosity of neighbors like you. Here's how you can make a difference.

## Donate

Veterinary costs add up quickly — each TNR visit includes trapping supplies, spay/neuter surgery, vaccinations, and follow-up care. Your donation goes directly toward the health and wellbeing of these cats.

<div class="callout">
  <strong>Donation options coming soon.</strong> In the meantime, reach out via the <a href="{{ '/pages/contact.html' | relative_url }}">contact page</a> if you'd like to contribute.
</div>

## Other Ways to Help

**Spread the word.** Share this site with neighbors. The more people who understand these cats are cared for and fixed, the safer the colony is.

**Don't relocate them.** These cats have been TNR'd and are being monitored. Moving them disrupts the colony and can undo years of work. If you're concerned about a specific cat, please [contact us]({{ '/pages/contact.html' | relative_url }}) first.

**Feed responsibly.** If you'd like to help feed the colony, please coordinate with the caretaker so feeding happens in consistent locations and at consistent times.

**Trap & report newcomers.** If you notice a cat that doesn't have an ear tip, it may be a new arrival that hasn't been TNR'd yet. Please [let us know]({{ '/pages/contact.html' | relative_url }}) so we can humanely trap and get them fixed.

**Sponsor a cat.** Help offset the cost of a specific cat's care. [Learn about sponsorship →]({{ '/pages/sponsor.html' | relative_url }})
